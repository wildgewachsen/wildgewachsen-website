2. Data Protection Officer

No data protection officer has been appointed. This website is operated by a single individual ("solo operator"). The conditions for the mandatory appointment of a DPO under Art. 37 GDPR and § 38 BDSG are not met (no large-scale regular monitoring, no special categories of data as core business, fewer than 20 persons engaged in automated processing).

3. Processing Purposes and Legal Bases

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Provision of the website (server logs): Technical data is collected when the site is accessed (IP address, date, time, browser type, operating system, referrer URL). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating and securing the website).
  • Audience measurement (GoatCounter): Anonymous, cookieless statistics on page views. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; additionally § 25 (2) (2) TDDDG, as no information is stored on or read from end-user devices beyond what is strictly necessary.
  • Newsletter (Buttondown): Processing of your e-mail address to send the newsletter (double opt-in). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent) and Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of the newsletter contract).
  • Affiliate tracking (Wise, SafetyWing, World Nomads, Booking.com): If you consent in the cookie banner, clicks on affiliate links are tracked via the relevant providers' cookies so that commissions can be attributed correctly. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG (explicit consent via the Klaro cookie banner).
  • Contact requests: Processing of the information you provide so that we can respond to your query. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual / contractual) or Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in communication).

4. Legitimate Interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR)

  • Server logs: maintaining the technical operability and security of the website against attacks and abuse.
  • Audience measurement: understanding which content reaches readers in order to improve the website editorially. We use GoatCounter, a tool that does not set cookies and does not store IP addresses.

6. Transfers to Third Countries

Some recipients are located outside the EEA. We safeguard such transfers as follows:

  • USA (Cloudflare, Buttondown): based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Commission adequacy decision of 10.07.2023, to the extent the providers are certified) and/or standard contractual clauses (SCC) pursuant to Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR.
  • United Kingdom (Wise): Commission adequacy decision of 28.06.2021.
  • Australia (World Nomads): transfer based on standard contractual clauses pursuant to Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR; no adequacy decision.
  • Turks and Caicos Islands (SafetyWing): transfer based on standard contractual clauses pursuant to Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR; no adequacy decision. An EU representative under Art. 27 GDPR has been appointed.

Transfers to third countries involve risks (e.g. broader access by state authorities, potential restrictions on enforcing data subject rights). You can object to processing by affiliate providers via the cookie banner.

7. Storage Periods

  • Server logs (Cloudflare): kept according to Cloudflare defaults; to our knowledge no longer than 7 days. Details: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Klaro cookie ("wildgewachsen-klaro"): 365 days from being set or until consent is withdrawn.
  • Affiliate cookies: storage period set by the respective provider; see the linked privacy policies.
  • Newsletter data: until unsubscribe. After unsubscribe the record is removed at Buttondown.
  • Contact requests: until the matter has been resolved or for as long as statutory retention obligations require.
